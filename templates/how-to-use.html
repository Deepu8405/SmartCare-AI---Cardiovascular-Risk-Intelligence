Project Documentation

Deep dive into the architecture, data, and logic behind SmartCare AI.

🌟 Motivation

Cardiovascular diseases are the leading cause of death globally. Most people find medical reports complex and screening inaccessible. SmartCare AI was born from the need to bridge the gap between clinical data and patient understanding through accessible technology.

🎯 Objective

To build a production-grade platform that quantifies heart risk using Ensemble ML and provides 20-feature clinical analysis supported by a RAG-enabled AI Assistant for medical report interpretation.

🌍 Real-World Significance

Early Detection

Identifies high-risk indicators before they lead to emergency cardiac events.

Health Literacy

The AI Agent explains complex PDF reports in simple language for non-medical users.

Data Tracking

Saves clinical records to Supabase to monitor heart health trends over months or years.

🛣️ Development Roadmap

Phase 1

Data Science

Training Random Forest & XGBoost models with 20 features.

Phase 2

Core Platform

Flask Backend & Supabase integration for Auth/Storage.

Phase 3

RAG AI Assistant

Integrating Gemini 1.5 to parse PDFs & analyze risk scores.

Phase 4

Deployment

Production-ready hosting with Gunicorn on Render.

📊 Data & Parameters

The model uses 20 key clinical parameters. Below are the most significant ones:

🫀

Had Angina / Stroke

History of chest pain or stroke are the highest weighted features.

🚭

Smoker Status

Categorical analysis of current vs. former smoking habits.

🛌

Sleep Hours

Abnormal sleep patterns (too low or too high) impact cardiac recovery.

🦷

Removed Teeth

Poor oral health is a proxy for systemic inflammation and heart risk.
